About optasy

A weekly view of injuries to the defenses your candidate starters face.

This prototype opens with fictional players, matchups and injuries. Start a new snapshot to enter your own candidates and reports, or import a saved snapshot. There is no automatic live feed.

Role links describe possible football mechanisms. They do not prove an easier matchup, measure replacement quality, or add fantasy points. Existing projections may already account for an injury.

Report ages over 48 hours are flagged for review, not treated as invalid. Reports timestamped at or after kickoff are excluded. A “reviewed” report is a user-supplied coverage label, not independent verification.

Nothing is uploaded or saved automatically. Imports and edits stay in memory until downloaded. No accounts, analytics or advertising.
